Islands We Have Shipped

Six representative projects covering UEFN map creation, Verse systems, performance rescue, compliance, thumbnails, and creator growth. Client names and exact figures are anonymized where required.

Every project below started with a measurable problem: a launch date that could not slip, a frame rate that collapsed on mobile, a leaderboard players refused to trust, or a channel stuck below the monetization threshold. We do not sell a template and call it custom. We scope the constraint, build the smallest system that solves it, test it under real player load, and hand over the source.

The case studies are presented in the same structure we use internally: the challenge we were hired to solve, the solution we shipped, and the result the client could verify. Use them as a starting point for your own brief — then tell us which constraint matters most for your island.

Image / video placeholder 01
UEFN Case Study 01 8-day build

Neon Rooftops — Parkour Free-For-All

Challenge

A creator needed a launch-ready parkour PvP island in eight days, on a tight memory budget, without reusing the same rooftop kit players had already seen in competing maps.

Solution

We built a modular rooftop kit with three height tiers, wired spawn protection and elimination scoring in Verse, and added a rotating hazard system that changes the safe route every round.

Result

Shipped two days early, held 60fps on console and PC, and crossed 180,000 plays in its first month with no runtime crashes reported.

Image / video placeholder 02
Verse Case Study 02 21-day build

Skyforge Kingdom — Persistent Tycoon

Challenge

The client wanted a tycoon economy that felt rewarding after ten hours, but their prototype lost all progress between sessions and the upgrade curve flattened after twenty minutes.

Solution

We rebuilt the economy around a Verse save-data layer, added tiered generators with soft and hard caps, and introduced a prestige loop that resets progress in exchange for permanent multipliers.

Result

Session persistence stabilized, average session length rose from 9 to 24 minutes, and the island reached a 62% day-one retention rate during its launch week.

VAULT
Image / video placeholder 03
Verse Case Study 03 12-day build

Speedrun Vault — Verified Leaderboards

Challenge

Players were skipping checkpoints and editing shortcuts into the course, so the leaderboard had become a list of exploits instead of legitimate runs.

Solution

We made checkpoint validation server-authoritative in Verse, added ordered-route enforcement and a replay flag for suspicious splits, and rebuilt the leaderboard to show verified personal bests only.

Result

Over 40,000 verified runs in the first six weeks, a 91% drop in disputed records, and a leaderboard the community began moderating on its own.

SIGNAL
Image / video placeholder 04
UEFN Case Study 04 18-day rescue

Hollow Signal — Co-op Boss Rush

Challenge

A four-player boss arena desynced whenever two players triggered the phase transition at once, causing health bars and attack patterns to disagree between clients.

Solution

We replaced the ad-hoc device wiring with a single Verse encounter controller that owns phase state, validates transition requests, and broadcasts one authoritative state to every player.

Result

Phase desyncs fell to zero in a 200-session test, crash reports dropped 74%, and the studio reused the controller as the foundation for its next two bosses.

RENDER
Image / video placeholder 05
Design Case Study 05 10-day sprint

Thumbnail System — 3D Render Pipeline

Challenge

The client was publishing strong islands with flat, low-contrast thumbnails. Click-through rate sat at 4.1%, so Discover impressions rarely turned into sessions.

Solution

We built a reusable 3D render pipeline with consistent lighting, depth, character framing, and bold text hierarchy, then produced three variants per island for an A/B test.

Result

The winning variant lifted click-through rate from 4.1% to 9.8%, and the template now lets the client ship a new thumbnail in under an hour.
