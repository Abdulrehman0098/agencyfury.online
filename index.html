Everything clients ask before they place a build order or file a growth campaign.

Express priority compresses that window to 7 to 10 days, and our super express tier delivers a playable island in as little as 5 days. Every timeline is written into your project agreement before work begins, and you receive a private build link with weekly progress updates so you always know exactly where your island stands. Rush slots are limited because we never stack more builds than the studio can finish on schedule.

Yes. Our IARC age rating service carries a 100 percent approval record across every island we have submitted. We audit your content against the current Epic Games and IARC questionnaire before filing, flag anything that could trigger a rejection, and handle the full submission and follow up process for you. If a rating is ever rejected because of our filing, we correct it and resubmit at no extra cost. You keep full ownership of the final rating certificate and the associated content descriptors for your island.

Our growth packages deliver real engagement from genuine accounts, never bot farms. You receive a private tracking link and a delivery report when the campaign closes.
