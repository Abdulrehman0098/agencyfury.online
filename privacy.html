Privacy Policy

Your privacy is our priority. This policy explains how FURY AGENCY collects, uses, and protects your information when you visit our website.

Last Updated: September 19, 2025

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy governs FURY AGENCY's ("we," "our," or "us") use of personal information collected through our website at agencyfury.online (the "Site"). By accessing or using the Site, you agree to the collection and use of information described in this policy. We are committed to safeguarding your privacy and complying with applicable data protection laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and other relevant legislation.

2. Information We Collect

We collect two types of information: Personal Information and Non-Personal Information. Personal Information may include your name, email address, phone number, and any other details you voluntarily provide through contact forms, newsletter subscriptions, or direct communication. Non-Personal Information includes technical data such as IP addresses, browser type, operating system, referral URLs, and usage patterns collected automatically when you interact with the Site.

3. Google AdSense & Advertising

This Site participates in the Google AdSense advertising program. Google AdSense serves advertisements on our behalf using cookies and web beacons. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this Site and other websites across the internet. This allows Google to display personalized advertisements that may be of interest to you.

We have implemented Google's ad personalization controls and comply with Google AdSense program policies. You can opt out of the use of cookies for ad personalization by visiting Google Ad Settings or by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on at https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

4. Google DART Cookies

Google uses the DART cookie to serve ads based on your visits to this Site and other sites on the internet. The DART cookie enables Google and its partners to serve targeted advertisements to you based on your browsing behavior. This includes, but is not limited to, pages you have visited, searches you have conducted, and content you have interacted with.

You have the right to opt out of the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google Advertising Opt-Out page at https://tools.google.com/dlpage/dopt. Additionally, you may choose to disable cookies through your browser settings, though this may affect your experience on the Site and other websites.

5. Third-Party Advertising

In addition to Google AdSense, this Site may display advertisements served by third-party ad networks, including but not limited to Meta Ads, TikTok Ads, and programmatic ad exchanges. These third-party advertisers may use cookies, web beacons, pixel tags, and other tracking technologies to collect information about your browsing activity across multiple websites. This data is used to build interest profiles and serve targeted advertisements.

FURY AGENCY does not have direct access to or control over the data collected by these third-party advertisers. Each third-party ad provider operates under its own privacy policy. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of all third-party ad networks that serve advertisements on this Site. For a comprehensive list of third-party ad partners and their opt-out mechanisms, please refer to the Digital Advertising Alliance's opt-out portal at https://optout.aboutads.info.

6. Analytics & Tracking Technologies

We use Google Analytics to analyze how visitors use the Site. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect information about your use of the website, including IP addresses, browser type, pages visited, time spent on pages, and exit pages. This data is transmitted to and stored by Google on servers located in the United States and other countries.

Google Analytics data helps us understand visitor behavior, improve content, and optimize the user experience. We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties except as described in this policy. Under the GDPR, you have the right to object to the processing of your data based on legitimate interests (including analytics). You may exercise this right by contacting us through the methods described below.

  • Right to Erasure: You may request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal exceptions.
  • Right to Restriction: You may request restriction of processing under certain conditions.
  • Right to Data Portability: You may request transfer of your data to another controller.
  • Right to Object: You may object to processing based on legitimate interests or direct marketing.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting prior processing.
