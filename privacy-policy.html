Privacy Policy

Your privacy is our priority. This policy explains how FURY AGENCY collects, uses, and protects your information when you visit our website.

Last Updated: September 19, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy governs FURY AGENCY's ("we," "our," or "us") use of personal information collected through our website at agencyfury.online (the "Site"). By accessing or using the Site, you agree to the collection and use of information described in this policy. We are committed to safeguarding your privacy and complying with applicable data protection laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and other relevant legislation.

2. Information We Collect

We collect two types of information: Personal Information and Non-Personal Information.

Personal Information may include your name, email address, phone number, and any other details you voluntarily provide through contact forms, newsletter subscriptions, or direct communication. We only collect personal information that you choose to share with us.

Non-Personal Information includes technical data such as IP addresses, browser type, operating system, referral URLs, device characteristics, and usage patterns collected automatically when you interact with the Site. This data helps us understand how visitors use our Site and improve the user experience.

3. Google AdSense & Advertising

This Site participates in the Google AdSense advertising program. Google AdSense serves advertisements on our behalf using cookies and web beacons. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this Site and other websites across the internet. This allows Google to display personalized advertisements that may be of interest to you.

Ad personalization: When personalized advertising is enabled, Google collects and uses data (such as cookies, device identifiers, and usage data) to build a profile of your interests and show you relevant ads. We comply with Google AdSense Program Policies, including the EU User Consent Policy.

You can opt out of the use of cookies for ad personalization by visiting Google Ad Settings or by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Where required by law (in particular for users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland), we obtain consent before serving personalized advertising. A cookie-consent banner is displayed on first visit so you can manage your preferences.

4. Third-Party Advertising

In addition to Google AdSense, this Site may display advertisements served by third-party ad networks and programmatic ad exchanges. These third-party advertisers may use cookies, web beacons, pixel tags, and other tracking technologies to collect information about your browsing activity across multiple websites. This data is used to build interest profiles and serve targeted advertisements.

FURY AGENCY does not have direct access to or control over the data collected by these third-party advertisers. Each third-party ad provider operates under its own privacy policy. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of all third-party ad networks that serve advertisements on this Site. For a comprehensive list of third-party ad partners and their opt-out mechanisms, please refer to the Digital Advertising Alliance's opt-out portal.

5. Analytics & Tracking Technologies

We use Google Analytics to analyze how visitors use the Site. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect information about your use of the website, including IP addresses, browser type, pages visited, time spent on pages, and exit pages. This data is transmitted to and stored by Google on servers located in the United States and other countries.

Google Analytics data helps us understand visitor behavior, improve content, and optimize the user experience. We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties except as described in this policy. Under the GDPR, you have the right to object to the processing of your data based on legitimate interests (including analytics). You may exercise this right by contacting us through the methods described below.

7. GDPR & Your Rights

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you are protected under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable local data protection laws. You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access: You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: You may request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Right to Erasure: You may request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal exceptions.
  • Right to Restriction: You may request restriction of processing under certain conditions.
  • Right to Data Portability: You may request transfer of your data to another controller.
  • Right to Object: You may object to processing based on legitimate interests or direct marketing.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting prior processing.
