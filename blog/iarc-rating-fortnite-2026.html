How to Get IARC Rating for Your Fortnite Island in 2026

You cannot publish a Fortnite Creative island without an IARC age rating. Here is the exact process, what triggers a rating change, and how to avoid rejection.

Why You Need an IARC Rating

The International Age Rating Coalition (IARC) powers the age rating system behind Fortnite Creative. Every island you publish must carry a rating so that Epic Games can match content to players' age and regional requirements. Without it, your map stays stuck in draft mode — invisible to the Discover feed and impossible to share publicly.

The good news: the rating is free, it only takes a few minutes, and it updates dynamically as your content changes. The bad news: mis-answer the questionnaire and you can end up with a rating that limits or blocks your island in certain regions, or you can trigger a compliance review.

The Questionnaire

When you attempt to publish, Fortnite presents a series of questions. These map your island's content to an age category. The categories you are most likely to see:

  • Interactive (E10+): Appropriate for ages 10 and up. Typical for most UEFN maps.
  • Teen (T): Mild violence, minimal blood, moderate language.
  • Mature (M): Strong violence, blood, or suggestive themes — heavily restricted on Fortnite Creative.

The questionnaire covers violence, blood, language, gambling, substances, and sexual content. Crucially, it also asks about user-generated content and in-game purchases or randomized items (loot boxes), both of which can raise the rating or add warnings.

Common Pitfalls & Rejections

Inconsistent Answers

The single most common failure is answering "no" to everything, then publishing an island with visible combat and damage. Epic's automated checks and player reports flag this, and you can get a compliance strike. Always answer for the most extreme content in your island.

Reference to Real Money

Advertising external purchases, crypto, or brand deals inside the island raises red flags. Keep monetization cues on your social channels, not on the island.

Copyrighted Media

Claiming fair use doesn't protect you. Music, characters, and logos you don't own can trigger a takedown during review. Use Fortnite's built-in licensed audio or the UEFN license-clearance workflows.

"A rejection isn't the end — it's feedback. Read the exact reason, adjust the content or the answers, and resubmit. Most islands publish on the second attempt."

After Approval

  • Your rating appears automatically on your island's public page.
  • Changing content later (adding blood we'll say) can require a re-questionnaire. Epic may prompt you when you modify devices or scripts.
  • Keep an eye on player reports — community reports can trigger a re-review even if you originally passed.
